# The Inbox page

What `page/inbox.html` does, for the one who builds it.

- The page is the signed Inbox, sheet 8 of comchatx `spikes/inbox-wide` (signed 2026-11-18), with the cards of the inbox round's sheet 1 (signed 2026-11-24). Every card stands in columns by the seller's two presses, 分组 / Grouping (type, urgency, date or unread) and 排序 / Ordering (the ways not grouped by). The default is by type, urgency inside, then the newest file. By type a column stands only where both kinds wait; by urgency or date every column stands, three side by side from 63rem.
- The presses and the columns are drawn in both views whatever the count of cards, at the head of the page at every width, and the page is one width from its first draw, so neither moves when cards come; with none ever, the Inbox's mark, a title and a line on how to have cx put something here stand in the middle of the page; once every card is done, the signed empty line stands under them; and with one a press stores the pick and moves nothing. The pair picked is written to `inbox.json` in the tab, `{"by":…,"in":…}`, only when the seller picks, and read beside the first read; a tab without it, or one it cannot read, has the default.
- `inbox-done.json` in the tab keeps each done card the page has read, by the time the board gives its file; it is written only when that set changes and never touches `inbox.json`. A later page reads such a file only once 已处理 is opened or the board brings that card, and a file given at another time is read at once; a list it cannot read, or one refused, is none, and the page reads every file. 已处理's files are read newest first in every folder, and while 已处理 waits for them it draws each part's rows as they come, with no line saying nothing is done. Not built: the list grows with the done cards (about 34 KB at a thousand); a file changed with its time unchanged stays out of sight until 已处理 is opened; a read the board refuses without end is asked again without end.
- 已处理 / Done stands in columns by the same two presses, a card's mark, what was done and the day at its foot, its older weeks folded in each column. The title search is kept.
- A front matter value is read whole on its line, cut at a line feed alone: a line or paragraph separator, or a lone carriage return, inside it is part of the value; a file with CRLF line ends reads as before.
- A card's `level` (1, 2 or 3; absent is 1) draws three bars, as many in ink as the level; its small card is the level's name, under the pointer and the keyboard's focus as the kit's label is, and Escape takes it away. A press on the bars, or ↓ on them, opens the level's menu there, the three levels with the one in force ticked; Escape or a press outside shuts it and the keyboard goes back to the bars. A level picked moves the card at once, the keyboard with it, and is written into the card's head as a mark is; a write that fails puts the card back as it was.
- A waiting card is one size at every width and in both languages, 268px where it asks (its answer box included) and 192px where it tells: its title, two lines at most, then its words as one paragraph, three lines (two on a card that asks), headings, lists and quotes run together; a table, code or a picture is read on the whole card. A long answer grows the box by one line, then it scrolls; a card's options scroll in their own box, its Submit kept at the foot. There is no 「展开」.
- At a card's corner, before 复制, 引用 / Quote, named 「引用“{title}”」, puts the whole card over the chat's message box as its quote (`cxBoard.quote` with `whole: true`, the card's file and its title cut to 80 characters, its title and words cut to 2,000), and sends nothing; the chat writes where it came from. It is drawn only while `context.quote` says a chat takes a quote, so not in a board's window of its own.
- At a card's corner 复制 / Copy puts its title and words on the clipboard and, for 1.5 seconds, is a tick named 已复制 / Copied, said to a screen reader too; where the board does not let the page write the clipboard nothing changes and nothing is said.
- A card's title is a press that opens the whole card on a tab of its own, `board.open({ file })` with the card's path in the tab; the board hands that tab the file by `on("file")`, whose `file` is the same path. A card that asks with options keeps the kit's question at its top, the press holding the question's words as the kit draws them at each pick and turn.
- A card not yet opened has a dot by its title and is named 「{title}，未读」 / "{title}, unread". Opening it at its title writes `inbox-opened.json` in the tab, each card's file with the time the board gave it then; only cx writing the card again brings its dot back, the page's own writes (a mark, a level, an answer, an undo) carry the opened card to its new time. The list another page wrote is read beside the first read and joined. Not built: the list keeps a card's line after the card is gone, a few dozen bytes a card.
- A card with `questions` (the platform's `comchatx.options/1` or `comchatx.confirm/1`, as JSON) is drawn by the kit's `Kit.questions`, one element a card for as long as its `questions:` reads the same as written, the order of a form's keys included, in one language, so a draw, or a rewrite of the card's words alone, keeps the picks; a send the board refuses leaves the questionnaire as the seller left it, their picks, own words and the last question, and says it was not sent. The picks are put back by their options' names, so two options of one name in one question cannot be told apart, there or in the answer itself. × dismisses the whole card; with every question skipped the card is dismissed and nothing is sent.
- Its answer is sent as any answer is, `board.send(words, { card })`, the words each question over its answer, a confirm's title over its yes or no, so cx reads what the seller agreed to. It is written as `done: answered`, `answer` (the words sent) and `answers` (the platform's `OptionAnswers` or `{ form, confirmed }`, as JSON), and only onto the questions answered: a file whose questions are others by the time it is marked stands as the disk has it.
- A saved `answers` is read only in the shape of its card's form, as the platform reads one; any other is none, and the card under 已处理 is drawn from `answer`, as one without `answers` is.
- A draw builds again only the cards whose file, press or drawn words changed; a press on 完成, 忽略 or Send takes that card's element out and leaves every other card the element it was. A read of the board's files is a draw like any other: the top bar's button, its presses, 已处理's rows and a card whose file is as it was stay the elements they were, even as the presses beside the button come or go, so a press held across it lands. A press on a card built anew between its down and its up is not taken; the seller presses again. Not built: a pick of grouping made by keys while 已处理 or 返回 is held down by the pointer draws the top bar anew, and that press is lost; it needs the two inputs at once.
- A card that asks is answered in the board's own message box (`cxBoard.box`), one a card for as long as the page lives, so its words, caret and selection stay across a draw; the page gives the placeholder and the field's name, and its `send` throws where the answer did not go, so the words stay in the box. What the page's own field did that the box does not: it was one line high; its Send was a word, 发送 / Send, in the page's primary style, where the box draws the chat's mark; Ctrl, ⌘ or Alt with Enter sent, where the box breaks the line; a line break pasted into it was a space, where the box keeps it and sends it; it carried `autocomplete="off"`.
- A second question card answered within five seconds of the first send is refused by the platform's guard on a page's sends, one send a press: the page says the answer was not sent and keeps the picks.
- A send that went through and a mark that failed leave the card waiting, and a second answer is a second message. A read already under way when a card is marked can draw it as waiting until the next read.
- The page draws a read once it is whole: until the first read is whole it draws nothing and sets no count, and while it reads again it keeps every card it holds and the count it set. A board's 重新加载 makes a new page, so its first read draws nothing until it is whole.
- A tab of more than 32 cards is read in parts: when the board says the page asks too often, the page waits a second and reads on.
- Marking a card reads its file and writes it back with the marks. Words written to it between that read and the write are lost.
- Every write of a card (a mark, a level, an answer, an undo) puts `written` in its head, the time the board gave the file before the page first wrote it, where the head holds none; the page orders, groups and dates a card by it, so a card the page wrote keeps its place and its time.
- The page changes only `done`, `doneAt`, `answer`, `answers`, `level` and `written` in a card's front matter, and writes `inbox.json`, `inbox-done.json` and `inbox-opened.json`. It writes the file with LF line ends and plain `---` fences, without a BOM.
