# The Inbox page

What `page/inbox.html` does, for the one who builds it.

- The page is the signed Inbox, sheet 8 of comchatx `spikes/inbox-wide` (signed 2026-11-18). Cards stand in this order: cx's newest card marked `reply: true`, its answer to the seller's last message; then the cards in columns by the seller's two presses, 分组 / Grouping (type, urgency or date) and 排序 / Ordering (the two ways not grouped by). The default is by type, urgency inside, then the newest file. By type a column stands only where both kinds wait; by urgency or date every column stands, three side by side from 63rem.
- The presses are drawn where two cards or more can change places, at the head of the page at every width; with none or one card there are no presses and no columns. The pair picked is written to `inbox.json` in the tab, `{"by":…,"in":…}`, only when the seller picks, and read beside the first read; a tab without it, or one it cannot read, has the default.
- `inbox-done.json` in the tab keeps each done card the page has read, by the time the board gives its file; it is written only when that set changes and never touches `inbox.json`. A later page reads such a file only once 已处理 is opened or the board brings that card, and a file given at another time is read at once; a list it cannot read, or one refused, is none, and the page reads every file. 已处理's files are read newest first in every folder, and while 已处理 waits for them it draws each part's rows as they come, with no line saying nothing is done. Not built: the list grows with the done cards (about 34 KB at a thousand); a file changed with its time unchanged stays out of sight until 已处理 is opened; a read the board refuses without end is asked again without end.
- 已处理 / Done stands in columns by the same two presses, a card's mark, what was done and the day at its foot, its older weeks folded in each column. The title search is kept.
- A front matter value is read whole on its line, cut at a line feed alone: a line or paragraph separator, or a lone carriage return, inside it is part of the value; a file with CRLF line ends reads as before.
- A card's `level` (1, 2 or 3; absent is 1) draws three bars, as many in ink as the level; its small card is the level's name, under the pointer and the keyboard's focus as the kit's label is, and Escape takes it away.
- A card with `questions` (the platform's `comchatx.options/1` or `comchatx.confirm/1`, as JSON) is drawn by the kit's `Kit.questions`, one element a card for as long as its `questions:` reads the same as written, the order of a form's keys included, in one language, so a draw, or a rewrite of the card's words alone, keeps the picks; a send the board refuses leaves the questionnaire as the seller left it, their picks, own words and the last question, and says it was not sent. The picks are put back by their options' names, so two options of one name in one question cannot be told apart, there or in the answer itself. × dismisses the whole card; with every question skipped the card is dismissed and nothing is sent.
- Its answer is sent as any answer is, `board.send(words, { card })`, the words each question over its answer, a confirm's title over its yes or no, so cx reads what the seller agreed to. It is written as `done: answered`, `answer` (the words sent) and `answers` (the platform's `OptionAnswers` or `{ form, confirmed }`, as JSON), and only onto the questions answered: a file whose questions are others by the time it is marked stands as the disk has it.
- A saved `answers` is read only in the shape of its card's form, as the platform reads one; any other is none, and the card under 已处理 is drawn from `answer`, as one without `answers` is.
- A draw builds again only the cards whose file, fold, press or drawn words changed, and measures 「展开」 for those and for every card when the page's width changes, showing or hiding it by what it measures; a press on 已读, 忽略 or Send takes that card's element out and leaves every other card the element it was. A read of the board's files is a draw like any other: the top bar's button, its presses, 已处理's rows and a card whose file is as it was stay the elements they were, even as the presses beside the button come or go, so a press held across it lands. A press on a card built anew between its down and its up is not taken; the seller presses again.
- A second question card answered within five seconds of the first send is refused by the platform's guard on a page's sends, one send a press: the page says the answer was not sent and keeps the picks.
- A send that went through and a mark that failed leave the card waiting, and a second answer is a second message. A read already under way when a card is marked can draw it as waiting until the next read.
- The page draws a read once it is whole: until the first read is whole it draws nothing and sets no count, and while it reads again it keeps every card it holds and the count it set. A board's 重新加载 makes a new page, so its first read draws nothing until it is whole.
- A tab of more than 32 cards is read in parts: when the board says the page asks too often, the page waits a second and reads on.
- Marking a card reads its file and writes it back with the marks. Words written to it between that read and the write are lost.
- The page changes only `done`, `doneAt`, `answer` and `answers` in a card's front matter, and writes `inbox.json` and `inbox-done.json`. It writes the file with LF line ends and plain `---` fences, without a BOM.
