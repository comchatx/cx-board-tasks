# Changelog

- 0.5.0: the cards of the inbox round's sheet 1 (signed 2026-11-24). A waiting card is one size, 268px where it asks and 192px where it tells, its title two lines and its words one paragraph of three lines (two where it asks), and 「展开」 is gone. 复制 / Copy at a card's corner puts its title and words on the clipboard. A press on a card's urgency bars, or ↓ on them, opens the level's menu, and the level picked is written into the card. A card not yet opened has a dot by its title until it is opened at its title, on a tab of its own; the cards opened are kept in `inbox-opened.json`, and 未读 / Unread is a fourth way to group and order. The tick is 完成 / Done. Each write of a card puts `written` in its head, the time it had before the page first wrote it, so a card the page wrote keeps its place and its time. An Inbox that has never held a card shows its mark, a title and a line in the middle of the page. The page draws a card's file the board hands it (`cxBoard.on("file")`) whole, on a tab of its own. 加急's lit bars are the theme's warning yellow (`--warn`) and 特急's its red.
- 0.4.1: no card has a place of its own. A card marked `reply: true` is no longer drawn alone above the columns: it stands in its column by the page's order, as any card does, and the key is read as any unknown key is. The skill no longer names it.
- 0.4.0: a card that asks is answered in the board's own message box, the chat's (`cxBoard.box`), in place of the page's one-line field and its 发送 / Send: several lines, Enter sends and Shift+Enter breaks the line, the control the chat's Send mark under the chat's name for it. The placeholder 回答 cx / Answer cx and the field's name 回答“{title}” stay the page's. An answer that is not sent keeps its words in the box, as before.

A draw, a read of the board's files too, builds again only what changed: a press on 已读, 忽略 or Send takes that card's element out and builds nothing else, a press held across a read lands on the top bar's button, 已处理's rows and a card whose file is as it was, and 「展开」 is measured for a card built anew and when the page's width or its columns change, a pick of grouping too, shown or hidden by what it measures. `inbox-done.json` keeps the done cards the page has read, apart from the grouping in `inbox.json`, so a later page reads a done file the board gives at the same time only once 已处理 is opened or the board brings that card; 已处理 reads its files newest first and draws those rows as they come.

- 0.3.5: the Inbox as signed on 2026-11-18 (comchatx `spikes/inbox-wide`, sheet 8). Two presses, 分组 / Grouping and 排序 / Ordering, at the head of the page where two cards or more can change places, the pair kept in `待办/inbox.json`; columns on their own ground, three side by side from 63rem; by default by type, urgency inside. A card's `level` (1 常规 / Routine, 2 加急 / Urgent, 3 特急 / Very urgent; none is routine) draws a mark of three bars with its name in a small card. 已处理 / Done stands in columns by the same presses, a card's state and day at its foot; the title search is kept and the underlines are gone. A card with `questions`, in the platform's option or confirm form, is answered by the kit's questionnaire (cx-board 0.3.8): its answer is sent as any answer is, each question over its answer, and kept as `answer` and `answers` on the questions it answers; a saved `answers` is read only in its form's shape, and a send the board refuses keeps the seller's picks and words, put back by their options' names, while the card's `questions:` reads the same as written, its keys' order included. Two options of one question with the same name cannot be told apart: after a send the board refuses, a single pick goes to the first of them and several are not kept, and the answer itself names the option by that name. A question holding a line or paragraph separator is read whole. The page draws a read once it is whole, so a reload shows no part of the cards and no falling count. The skill says so.

- 0.3.0: the Inbox, designed from zero and signed on 2026-11-11.
