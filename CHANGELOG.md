# Changelog

- 0.3.4: the Inbox draws a card's words as cx's answers in the chat are, through the board's `cxBoard.markdown` (cx-board 0.3.6): bold, lists, tables, code and links, a link opening nothing; while the drawing comes, and where the board has no such ask or refuses it, they stand as paragraphs, as before. The skill and its example say so. The skill no longer says a question asked through pi's question box is the chat's: a question can be a card, which the turn does not wait on. The answer field's placeholder follows the page's language when it changes.
- 0.3.2: the skill says how the Inbox draws a card's words: plain paragraphs, a blank line between them, any other line break a space, and marks of formatting printed as typed.
- 0.3.2: the Inbox asks for the board when a read brings a waiting card it did not hold, by the card's file name and never on its first read, so the column opens on the Inbox through the board's door. The skill says so.
- 0.3.2: 已读, 忽略 and a sent answer move the card at the press, and the file is read and written behind it; a mark that is not written brings the card back as it was, and an answer that is not sent comes back with its words and 回答没有发出去。 / Your answer was not sent.
- 0.3.2: the Inbox reads the cards it does not hold 16 at once, where it read them one after another, so it draws a tab's cards after a few reads' time; a read the frame or the board refuses for too many asks is asked again a second later.
- 0.3.2: an answer that was sent and whose mark was not written leaves its card where its file says, waiting.
- 0.3.1: with this plugin, what cx has for the seller is a card in the Inbox, as the signed states draw it: the answer to what the seller asked, a small one too, a finding, a change, a proposal or a question. The chat shows the card by its title and where it stands, with no copy of its words. The skill's description says so in place of "Use when you leave the seller something to decide or to read on the board", which made the card cx's choice; its first sentence, the signed one, is unchanged. The skill says where the seller reads, each card on its own and later, and that cx's steps and a question asked in the turn stay in the chat.

- 0.3.0: the Inbox, designed from zero and signed on 2026-11-11. The package declares its own page for the tab `待办` (`comchatx.board`, `page/inbox.html`), named 收件箱 / Inbox, and the platform's native module gives way to it. A card is any Markdown file under `待办/`, with `title` and `asks: true` where it asks; the seller answers it in its own field, ticks one that tells, or dismisses it, and where it stands is written into its own front matter (`done`, `doneAt`, `answer`), nothing moved. Each answer goes to cx as it is sent, naming its card. The card's name is "Board Inbox"; the card's line, the skill's row and both READMEs are the lines signed with it. Files from 0.2 read as cards, under `todo/` and `done/` alike.
- 0.2.2: the card's name is the one signed on 2026-11-11, "Board To-dos", in `comchatx.name`, the same on the Chinese and the English page; the README, in English and Chinese, calls each of our plugins by its card's name, not its npm name.
- 0.2.1: The card's line and the Overview, in English and Chinese, are the words signed on 2026-11-10: "Lists what needs your decision, and sends cx every answer." as npm's `description`, and 「要你决定的事列进待办，答完一次交给 cx。」 as `comchatx.about`. They said "Board to-dos you read or answer, then find under Done." and 「看板上的待办：已读、已处理、历史，和要你回答的建议、选择、填写。」. `DEVELOPING.md` holds the install line, `pi install npm:@comchatx/cx-board-tasks`. `comchatx.builds` names `@comchatx/cx-board` as required, so the Plugins page installs the board with the module.
- 0.2.0: the module owns every state of the board: the places `todo/` and `done/`, 已读 and 忽略, and the history, all taken from `@comchatx/cx-board` 0.3.0, which keeps the directory alone. A question cx can ask now is asked in the turn; an entry is for a question that has to wait. `todo/` is the seller's, not cx's list of steps. The module lives in a tab of its own, the directory `待办`, and its places, kinds and answers are only there.
